# Medizo --- Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** November 20, 2026

Medizo ("we," "our," or "the app") provides medicine information,
general health education, health tools, and AI-assisted guidance. This
Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, and
handled when you use Medizo.

## 1. Information We May Collect

Depending on the features you use and how Medizo is configured, we may
collect or process:

-   **Account information:** such as email address or account
    identifiers, if you create an account.
-   **App activity and preferences:** such as saved/favorite medicines,
    settings, searches, or interactions with app features.
-   **Health-related information you provide:** such as questions,
    symptoms, or other health details you voluntarily enter into
    medicine-information features or the AI assistant.
-   **Device and technical information:** such as device type, operating
    system, app version, diagnostic logs, and basic usage data.
-   **Support communications:** information you provide when contacting
    us for help.

We do not ask you to provide more personal information than is needed to
use a feature. Please avoid submitting sensitive personal details unless
they are necessary for your request.

## 2. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

-   Provide and operate Medizo's features.
-   Display medicine information and health education content.
-   Respond to prompts submitted to the AI-assisted feature.
-   Save preferences or favorites where supported.
-   Maintain, troubleshoot, secure, and improve the app.
-   Respond to support requests.
-   Meet legal obligations and protect the app, its users, and our
    rights.

## 3. AI-Assisted Health Guidance

Medizo may provide AI-assisted responses to health-related questions.
Your prompt and relevant context may be processed by the AI service
provider to generate a response.

AI responses can be inaccurate, incomplete, or outdated. They are for
general informational and educational purposes only and are **not a
diagnosis, medical advice, or a substitute for a licensed healthcare
professional**. Do not rely on the AI assistant for emergencies,
treatment decisions, medication dosing, or to delay seeking professional
care. For urgent or emergency concerns, contact local emergency services
or a qualified healthcare professional.

Do not submit information that you do not want processed by the AI
service. The AI service provider's own privacy terms may also apply to
processing performed by that provider.

## 4. Sharing and Service Providers

We may use service providers to operate features of Medizo, such as
hosting, databases, authentication, AI processing, analytics, crash
reporting, notifications, or advertising---**only where those services
are actually integrated and enabled**.

Information may be shared with these providers as necessary to deliver a
feature, maintain the app, protect users, or comply with law. We do not
sell your personal information.

Before release, identify the service providers actually used in the app
and link to their privacy policies where required.

## 5. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary for the
purposes described in this policy, to provide app features, comply with
legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements. Retention
periods depend on the type of information and how the relevant feature
is implemented.

Where data deletion is available, you may request deletion using the
contact information below. Some information may need to be retained
where required by law or for legitimate security purposes.

## 6. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures intended to
protect information. However, no method of electronic storage or
transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute
security.

## 7. Your Choices and Requests

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to
request access to, correction of, deletion of, or restriction of certain
personal information.

You may also choose not to provide information requested by optional
features, although some features may then be unavailable. To make a
privacy-related request, contact us using the details below.

## 8. Children's Privacy

Medizo is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from
children where parental consent is required. If you believe a child has
provided personal information inappropriately, contact us so we can
review and take appropriate action.

## 9. Third-Party Links and Services

The app may contain links to external websites, medicine resources, or
third-party services. Those services are governed by their own privacy
policies and practices. We are not responsible for the privacy practices
of third-party websites or services.

## 10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be
posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use
of Medizo after an update means the revised policy is available for
review.

## Health Information Disclaimer

Medizo provides general medicine information, health education, and
AI-assisted informational guidance. Content is not intended to replace
professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Always seek advice
from a qualified healthcare professional regarding a medical condition
